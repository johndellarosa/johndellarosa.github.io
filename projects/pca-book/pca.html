Definition

Enter x, y, z observations. The colored arrows show the principal axes through the sample mean; arrow lengths are proportional to the standard deviations along them. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Drag: rotate · Scroll: zoom · Double-click: reset view
Data pointsPC1PC2PC3
Method: PCA eigendecomposition of the sample covariance matrix, S = XᵀX / (n−1) after centering (and optional standardization). Axes are orthonormal; signs of eigenvectors are arbitrary. Eigenvalues are sorted descending. The plotted points are always mean-centered, and are additionally scaled to unit sample variance when standardization is enabled.
