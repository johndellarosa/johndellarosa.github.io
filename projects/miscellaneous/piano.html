Wait for me: play the highlighted note, or build a chord one key at a time; release all keys to check. Wrong attempts stay on the same target. Steady tempo: play each highlighted chord together near its onset; duration is not graded.

Click a beat on the staff, then play notes to build a chord—even one key at a time. Click another position to save the current chord automatically, or use Place note / chord. Click a selected note again to remove it from the chord. Placement replaces any notes overlapping the chosen duration.

Press Start recording and follow the moving red line. Each dotted vertical guide marks a beat. Notes appear immediately and grow in duration as you hold them. Timing is rounded to the nearest eighth note. A held key determines its duration; pauses create rests. Chords share a stem when played together. Tip: Set BPM before recording.
