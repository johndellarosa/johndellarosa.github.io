Press Start recording and follow the moving red line. Each dotted vertical guide marks a beat. Notes appear immediately and grow in duration as you hold them. Timing is rounded to the nearest eighth note. A held key determines its duration; pauses create rests. Chords share a stem when played together. Tip: Set BPM before recording.
